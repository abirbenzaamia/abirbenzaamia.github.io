I am Ph.D. candidate at Ecole Polytechnique supervised by [Oana Goga](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/Labo/Oana.GOGA/), working at the intersection of *web privacy*, *online advertising*, and *algorithmic transparency*. I study the privacy–utility trade-offs of privacy-preserving solutions, particularly in advertising, by combining user-centered online surveys with large-scale web measurement. I also evaluate the transparency mechanisms of online services and their compliance with regulations such as the GDPR and the DSA, to support both users and auditors.
